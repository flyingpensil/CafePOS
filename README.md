## 2. UAE Statutory Tax Compliance (Critical FTA Standards)

### A. 5% Value Added Tax (VAT) Calculation
Under UAE Executive Regulations (Article 27):
1. **Mandatory Tax-Inclusive Pricing:** Retail shops and Cafeterias quoting prices to consumers ($P_{inc}$) must display prices inclusive of the standard 5% VAT.
2. **Statutory Tax Breakdown Formula:**
   $$\text{Taxable Subtotal (Net Base)} = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{Gross Amount}}{1.05}, 2\right)$$
   $$\text{Line VAT (5\%)} = \text{Gross Amount} - \text{Taxable Subtotal}$$
3. **Rounding Rules:** Calculations are performed per line item and aggregated at invoice level to 2 decimal places in AED (United Arab Emirates Dirham, Fils).

### B. Simplified Tax Invoice Requirements
Every receipt generated by the engine includes:
- Prominently centered heading: **"TAX INVOICE" / "فاتورة ضريبية"**
- Supplier Legal Registered Name in English & Arabic: `AL BARAKAH MODERN TRADING LLC` / `شركة البركة للتجارة الحديثة ذ.م.م`
- Seller 15-digit Tax Registration Number (**TRN / الرقم الضريبي**): e.g. `100458923100003`
- Branch Address and Contact Telephone in Dubai / UAE.
- Idempotent Sequential Invoice Number: e.g. `INV-DXB01-POS01-20261029-0042`
- Date & Time of supply in GST (Gulf Standard Time, GMT+4).
- Detailed line items with modifiers and line totals.
- Isolated Net Taxable Amount, 5% VAT Amount, and Gross Total in AED.

### C. UAE FTA TLV (Tag-Length-Value) Base64 QR Code Standard
The QR code is encoded using binary TLV format according to official GCC and FTA e-invoicing standards:
- **Tag 1 (0x01):** Seller Legal Trading Name (UTF-8 bytes)
- **Tag 2 (0x02):** Seller 15-digit TRN (ASCII string)
- **Tag 3 (0x03):** Invoice Timestamp in ISO 8601 format (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ssZ`)
- **Tag 4 (0x04):** Invoice Total in AED (inclusive of VAT, e.g. `24.50`)
- **Tag 5 (0x05):** VAT Total in AED (e.g. `1.17`)
- **Tag 6 & 7 (Optional for Phase 2):** Cryptographic SHA-256 invoice hash and ECDSA digital signature.

*The concatenated byte stream is Base64-encoded to generate the QR code.*

## 3. Core Operational Modes

### A. Cafeteria / Small Restaurant Mode
- **Touch-Optimized Quick-Order Grid:** Visual categories (`Hot Drinks`, `Sandwiches & Paratha`, `Shawarma & Grills`, `Fresh Juices`) with quick-tap cards.
- **Modifier Engine:** Supports single-choice (Sugar: Normal, Less, Sada) and multi-choice add-ons (Extra Cardamom +0.50 AED, Extra Kraft Cheese +1.50 AED).
- **Table Tabs & Parked Tickets:** Walk-in, Takeaway, and Dine-in table tabs (`T-01` to `T-08`) with instant Bill Split calculator.
- **Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT):** Auto-dispatching of kitchen tickets to ESC/POS thermal printers with item quantities and kitchen notes.

### B. Stationery / Retail Shop Mode
- **High-Speed Barcode Wedge Listener:** Listens globally to USB & Bluetooth scanners without requiring UI focus lag via inter-keystroke delta threshold (<50ms).
- **Loose Copy & Print Service Calculator:** Instant rate calculator for Photocopy B&W (0.25 AED/page), Color Laser Print (1.00 AED/page), Thermal Lamination A4 (2.50 AED), and Binding (7.00 AED).
- **Stock Control:** Min/Max stock thresholds with real-time low-stock (`LOW!`) warning badges at checkout.

## 4. Hardware & Peripheral Integration

- **Thermal Receipt Printers (80mm & 58mm):** Generates native ESC/POS binary buffers:
  - Initialize: `0x1B, 0x40` (`ESC @`)
  - Center/Left/Right alignment: `0x1B, 0x61, n`
  - Font Sizing & Double-Height: `0x1D, 0x21, n`
  - Paper cut with feed: `0x1D, 0x56, 0x01`
- **Cash Drawer Kick:** 24V pulse triggered upon cash settlement:
  - Standard command: `0x1B, 0x70, 0x00, 0x19, 0xFA` (`ESC p 0 25 250`)
- **Keyboard Shortcuts:**
  - `F1`: Focus search / barcode
  - `F2`: Park / Hold active cart
  - `F3`: Recall held order / Open tabs modal
  - `F4`: Fast Exact Cash
  - `F5`: Pay / Open Payment modal
  - `F8`: Discount modal (Supervisor PIN 9999)
  - `F9`: Void / Clear cart
  - `F10`: Open Cash Drawer
  - `F11`: Switch Operational Mode (Cafeteria <-> Stationery)

## 5. Offline Sync & Shift Management

- **Deterministic Collision-Proof Numbering:**
  `INV-[BRANCH_CODE]-[TERMINAL_ID]-[YYYYMMDD]-[SEQUENCE_0001]`
  Guarantees that multiple offline terminals at the same branch never generate collision invoice numbers even after days without internet.
- **Chained Hash Ledger:** Tamper-evident ledger hashing previous invoice hash with current invoice payload.
- **Shift Reconciliation (X & Z Reports):**
  - **X-Report:** Non-resettable mid-shift reading: Opening float + Cash sales + Cash In - Cash Out = Expected Cash.
  - **Z-Report:** End-of-day closure with physical counted cash and Over/Short discrepancy calculation.
